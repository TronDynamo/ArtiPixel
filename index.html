What pals can do

Small pal, big toolbox.

Every pal comes equipped with these skills. Choose what each one uses in its Skills tab in the workshop. Skills marked "Try it here" already work in this preview's chat; open the platform menu and ask.

Errands

Put your pals to work.

Give any companion a job and a schedule. It delivers your notifications in person, checks your accounts, and runs errands you describe in plain words. Several pals with their own errands make a little automation farm.

    Available now

    Put a pal on your desktop

    For Windows 10 and 11. Saves a small zip; open it and double-click Start [name], and your pal appears on your real desktop. They walk along the taskbar and the tops of your windows, ride a window when you move it, and nap if you leave them alone. Drag them anywhere and let go to drop them. Click to chat. Right-click (or their icon by the clock) to nap, stay put, start with Windows, or say goodbye. Nothing gets installed.

    The first time, Windows may say it protected your PC: choose More info, then Run anyway. The file also carries your pal’s sprite sheet, soul and settings, so the full desktop app can import them later.
